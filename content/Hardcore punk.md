### Subgenres and Fusion Genres
- [[Metalcore]] (fusion with metal)
- [[Mathcore]] (fusion with math rock)
- [[Post hardcore]] (evolution with more melody/experimentation)
- [[Sludge metal]] (fusion with Doom metal)
- [[Crossover thrash]] (fusion with Thrash metal)
- [[Crust punk]] (fusion with extreme metal)
- [[Uk 82]] (fusion with NWOBHM)
### Description
Hardcore punk is a fast, aggressive subgenre of punk rock, known for its short songs, shouted vocals, and DIY ethic.
