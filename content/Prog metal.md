### Subgenres and Fusion Genres
- [[Djent]] (+ rhythm complexity)
- [[Avant-garde metal]] (fusion with experimental/other genres)
- [[Progressive death metal]] (fusion with Death metal)
### Description
Progressive metal combines the complexity and experimentation of progressive rock with the power and aggression of heavy metal, often featuring odd time signatures and virtuosic musicianship.
