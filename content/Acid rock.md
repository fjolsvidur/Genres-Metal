### Description
Acid rock is a subgenre of psychedelic rock defined by heavy, distorted guitars, extended improvisation, and surreal lyrics, often associated with the late 1960s counterculture.
### Example Bands
- The Jimi Hendrix Experience
- Iron Butterfly
- Blue Cheer
- Jefferson Airplane
- Quicksilver Messenger Service
- The Electric Prunes
- The 13th Floor Elevators
- Big Brother and the Holding Company
- Country Joe and the Fish
- Vanilla Fudge
