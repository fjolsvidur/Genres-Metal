### Subgenres and Fusion Genres
- [[Crossover thrash]] (fusion with Hardcore punk)
### Description
Thrash metal is a fast, aggressive subgenre of heavy metal characterized by rapid guitar riffs, double bass drumming, and socially conscious or rebellious lyrics.

- Slayer
- Megadeth
