### Subgenres and Fusion Genres
- [[Blackgaze]] (fusion with shoegaze)
- [[Post-black metal]] (fusion with Post-metal)
- [[Avant-garde metal]] (fusion with progressive/experimental)
- [[Symphonic black metal]] (fusion with symphonic/orchestral elements)
### Description
Black metal is an extreme subgenre of heavy metal characterized by fast tempos, shrieking vocals, heavily distorted guitars, raw recording, and an emphasis on atmosphere and anti-mainstream themes.
