### Subgenres and Fusion Genres
- [[Symphonic power metal]] (fusion with symphonic/orchestral elements)
### Description
Power metal is a subgenre of heavy metal known for its uplifting melodies, fast tempos, fantasy-themed lyrics, and soaring vocals.

- Blind Guardian
- DragonForce
