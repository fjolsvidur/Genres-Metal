### Subgenres and Fusion Genres
- [[Deathcore]] (fusion with Death metal)
- [[Mathcore]] (fusion with math rock)
### Description
Metalcore is a **fusion of extreme metal and hardcore punk**, known for its breakdowns, melodic riffs, and a mix of screamed and clean vocals.
