Roots in garage rock and rock and roll, punk is characterized by short, fast-paced songs, hard-edged melodies, and anti-establishment lyrics.

### Description
Punk is a rock music genre that emerged in the mid-1970s, known for its raw sound, DIY ethic, and rebellious attitude.

### Genres
| Genre           | Creation Time | Golden Era       |
|-----------------|--------------|------------------|
| Punk rock       | Mid-1970s    | Late 1970s-1980s |
| Hardcore punk   | Late 1970s   | 1980s            |
| Post-hardcore   | Mid 1980s    | 2000s            |
| Mathcore        | Late 1990s   | 2000s            |
