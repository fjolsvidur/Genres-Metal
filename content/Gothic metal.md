### Subgenres and Fusion Genres
- [[Symphonic metal]] (fusion with symphonic/orchestral music)
- [[Symphonic gothic metal]] (fusion with symphonic/orchestral elements)
### Description
Gothic metal blends the heaviness of doom and death metal with dark, atmospheric elements and often features both male and female vocals, drawing inspiration from gothic rock.

- Type O Negative
- Tristania
- Theatre of Tragedy
- Moonspell
