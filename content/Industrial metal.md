### Subgenres and Fusion Genres
- [[Nu-metal]] (fusion with rap/alternative rock)
### Description
Industrial metal blends elements of industrial music and heavy metal, featuring repetitive metal guitar riffs, sampling, synthesizers, and distorted vocals.
### Example Bands
- Ministry
- Rammstein
- Fear Factory
- Nine Inch Nails
- Godflesh
- Static-X
- Marilyn Manson
- KMFDM
- Rob Zombie
- Oomph!
- Strapping Young Lad
