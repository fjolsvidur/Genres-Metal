### Subgenres and Fusion Genres
- [[Melodic death metal]] (fusion with melody/harmony)
- [[Deathcore]] (fusion with Metalcore)
- [[Progressive death metal]] (fusion with Prog metal)
- [[Death-doom]] (fusion with Doom metal)
- [[Avant-garde metal]] (fusion with progressive/experimental)
- [[Symphonic death metal]] (fusion with symphonic/orchestral elements)
### Description
Death metal is an extreme subgenre of heavy metal known for its heavily distorted guitars, growled vocals, blast beat drumming, and complex song structures.
