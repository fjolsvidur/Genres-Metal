### Subgenres and Fusion Genres
[[Stoner-doom]] (fusion with Doom metal)
### Description
Stoner rock is a subgenre of rock music that combines elements of psychedelic rock, blues rock, and doom metal, characterized by slow-to-mid tempos, heavy riffs, and a 'fuzzy' sound.

- Queens of the Stone Age
