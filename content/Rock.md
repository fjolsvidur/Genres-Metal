Rock music originated in the 1950s and evolved from rock and roll, rhythm and blues, and country music, becoming one of the most popular and diverse genres.

### Description
Rock is a broad genre of popular music that emphasizes electric guitars, strong rhythms, and memorable melodies, with countless subgenres and styles.

### Genres
| Genre            | Creation Time | Golden Era       |
| ---------------- | ------------- | ---------------- |
| Rock and roll    | 1950s         | 1950s-1960s      |
| Blues rock       | Mid 1960s     | Late 1960s-1970s |
| Psychedelic rock | Mid 1960s     | Late 1960s       |
| Acid rock        | Late 1960s    | Late 1960s-1970s |
| Progressive rock | Late 1960s    | 1970s            |
| Hard rock        | Late 1960s    | 1970s-1980s      |
| Stoner rock      | Early 1990s   | 1990s-2000s      |
| Punk rock        | Mid-1970s     | Late 1970s-1980s |
| Alternative rock | 1980s         | 1990s            |
