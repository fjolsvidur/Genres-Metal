### Description
Blues rock is a fusion genre combining elements of blues and rock, featuring electric guitar riffs, bluesy improvisation, and a strong rhythm section.
### Example Bands
- Cream
- The Rolling Stones
- Stevie Ray Vaughan and Double Trouble
- The Yardbirds
- ZZ Top
- The Allman Brothers Band
- Eric Clapton
- Rory Gallagher
- Joe Bonamassa
- Gary Moore
