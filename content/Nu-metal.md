### Description
Nu-metal is a subgenre of alternative metal that **combines elements of heavy metal with hip hop, grunge, and alternative rock**, often featuring downtuned guitars and rapped vocals.
### Example Bands
- Korn
- Limp Bizkit
- Linkin Park
- Slipknot
- Deftones
- Papa Roach
- Disturbed
- Mudvayne
- P.O.D.
- Static-X
- Coal Chamber
