### Subgenres and Fusion Genres
- [[Gothic metal]]
### Description
Death-doom is a fusion of death metal and doom metal, combining slow tempos and melancholic atmosphere with growled vocals and heavy riffs.
### Example Bands
- Paradise Lost
- My Dying Bride
- Anathema
- Novembers Doom
- Swallow the Sun
- Katatonia
- Draconian
- Mourning Beloveth
- Hooded Menace
- Evoken
